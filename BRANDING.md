# Branding Guidelines

## Product Name

**AgentOpsHub** is the product name for this fork.

## Upstream Attribution

This product is based on **Paseo** (https://github.com/getpaseo/paseo), licensed under Apache-2.0.
The factual attribution "AgentOpsHub is based on the Apache-2.0 Paseo project" is acceptable and required.

## What is NOT permitted

- Using "Paseo" as the AgentOpsHub product name, app display name, CLI command, or release identity.
- Using Paseo logos, icons, or trademarks to represent AgentOpsHub.
- Implying AgentOpsHub is the official Paseo project or affiliated with the Paseo maintainers.
- Removing or obscuring the upstream Apache-2.0 LICENSE, copyright headers, or legally required notices.

## What IS permitted

- Stating "AgentOpsHub is derived from Paseo" or "based on the Apache-2.0 Paseo project."
- Retaining Paseo references in internal protocol fields, storage keys, and configuration for compatibility.
- Using internal identifiers that reference Paseo in backward-compatible paths during transition.
